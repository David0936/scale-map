# Media Relay

The Style Bible says the visual language evolves with time. For a film this becomes a **relay**: each era is drawn in its own medium, and the baton passes at era boundaries. This table maps each era to a code renderer and an entry transition from huashu-art-motion.

## Default relay for household products (four media)

Used by EP01. The production cost is about 1.3× a single-style film.

| Segment | Era | Medium | What the medium must show | Renderer / starting point | Entry transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HOOK + NOW/AFTER | today | Contemporary flat illustration with print grain | the device, the room, the routine | `SM.F` palette + `SM.grain`; huashu `16_2026` as material | `iris` (into the present) |
| BEFORE | pre-industrial | Chinese ink wash (水墨写意) | the bent body, the hand tool, the endless task | `PAINT.brush` / `PAINT.leaf`; huashu `17_ink` | `inkBloom` |
| TURNING POINT | 1850–1920 | Newspaper copper engraving / patent plate | mechanism, scale, crowds, infrastructure | `SM.hatch / tone / solid` + `SM.boiled` line boil | `pageTurn` |
| EVOLUTION | 1950–2005 | Consumer advertising poster | aspiration, price, mass adoption | flat fill offset from the ink line (misregistration) + halftone dots; huashu `13_pop` as material | `dotBloom` |

The thread across all four media: one six-color palette (EP01: `#2a2118 #ece2c9 #c8402e #e8a93a #2f5d50 #f6efe2`), the same object (the cleaning machine) and in-world text.

## Alternatives

| Direction | When to choose | Cost | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Full relay** (6–7 media: painting → engraving → Bauhaus → poster → product photo → UI → AI surreal) | the flagship episode; strongest fit with the Style Bible | about 2× | Every weak medium shows as collage. Do it only when each medium reaches three stars in huashu's `风格配方/INDEX.md`. |
| **Patent plates throughout** | a recurring series look; product-mechanism stories | 1× | One renderer; lines draw themselves on and callouts animate. Each machine generation gets one plate. |
| **Isometric flat cutaways** | explaining how a product moved through rooms (SPACE layer) | 1× | Rejected as EP01's master style because it looked like a toy and lost historical weight. Fine as a single shot. |

## Choosing a medium for an era

Pick the medium that **people of that era used to show this kind of thing to each other**: 1901 engineering was reported in illustrated newspapers, and 1950s convenience was sold through posters and TV. If an era's medium does not explain the claim, use the neighbouring era's medium rather than adding a fifth.

Check huashu's `references/风格配方/INDEX.md` for the current quality rating (★–★★★) and known weaknesses of each style card before relying on it.
